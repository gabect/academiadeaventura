# Mountain Studio v0.50.0 – illustrated ecology density and vertical cliff cover

## Changes
- Adds reproducible pockets of painted fern, grass and broadleaf sprites along the actual cliff foot, preserving route buffers, water, gameplay center and steep-grade exclusions.
- Attaches vines and roots to geologically solved rock-face coordinates; adds hanging grass caps to crest and existing true stone ledges.
- Uses 12 alpha-tested and depth-written illustrated card groups (ground + cliff) with low/medium/high instance density, no extra physics meshes.
- Increases botanical sprite height variation to fit character scale. Fallback organic geometry remains until primary grass sprite loads.
- Rebuilds conifer crowns from ten staggered tiers with two crossed frond surfaces per branch to reduce flat floating discs.
- Keeps normal mode rendering and collision unchanged.
- Adds CI scenery site-count assertions and a cliff-foot close view; screenshots continue to be reviewable before deployment.

## Remaining limitations
These art images were extracted from illustrated concept art; not truly commissioned 3D models or large seamless source textures. Visual acceptance in a real mobile browser and FPS must still be evaluated. No claim of matching the target illustration.
